Privacy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Meridian has no accounts, no logins, and no server of its own. Your game data never leaves your browser.

What is stored, and where

The game keeps your progress in your own browser, using localStorage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data erases all of it permanently, and there is no copy to restore from.

Under the key meridian.v2.state it stores:

  • Today's board for each difficulty — your guesses so far and whether the round is finished. This is what lets a refresh restore the game rather than lose it, and what keeps a finished round finished.
  • Per-difficulty statistics: games played and won, streaks, your best streak, the spread of how many guesses you needed, and which milestones you have reached.
  • Preferences: your last-used tab, and whether you asked the instructions not to show again.
  • Results from the last 90 days, so the calendar strip can be drawn. Older days are pruned automatically.

A key from an earlier version, meridian:<difficulty>, may also be present. It holds only old statistics and is read once so your history carries over.

What is not collected

  • No account, email address, or name.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or third-party scripts.
  • No cookies. The game sets none.
  • No location data. Your time zone is read from your device to show your own clock beside the puzzle's, and that reading stays on the page.
  • Nothing is sent to any server. There is no server to send it to.

Things outside our control

Two requests do leave your browser, and each is governed by that provider's own privacy policy rather than this one:

  • Typefaces load from Google Fonts. The game works without them, falling back to your system fonts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server logs including IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing your result

The share button copies a short text summary to your clipboard. Nothing is posted anywhere. Where it goes next is entirely up to you.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Should analytics or advertising ever be added, this page will say so before they go live.
