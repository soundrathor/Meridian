How this works

The clock above shows the real local time in one mystery city. That is your only clue to begin with. Every wrong guess opens the next one — hemisphere, region, population, climate, then a fact about the place. You get six guesses. Each guess also reports the distance and bearing to the target, so you can close in even when you have no idea. Populations are city-proper figures.
